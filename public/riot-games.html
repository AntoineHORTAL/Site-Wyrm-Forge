For Riot Games — product key review

What's actually in Wyrm Forge, right now

Wyrm Forge is a companion site and desktop overlay for League of Legends. This page is a plain, current snapshot of the product for anyone reviewing our production API key request: what's live, what's built but not yet switched on, what we're actively working on, and what's still just an idea.

Stage 1 of 4 — shipped

Forged

Live in production today, on the website and inside the desktop app.

Website — wyrm-forge.com

  • Bilingual site & dashboardMarketing pages and player dashboard, in French and English.
  • Player & match searchLook up a summoner and browse recent match history.
  • Subscription tiersA free tier plus two paid tiers, with card payments handled through Stripe.

Desktop companion app

  • In-game overlayLive stats and item timings, read from Riot's Live Client Data API while a game is running.
  • Comeback trackerReads how a game is trending and flags momentum swings.
  • Build assistantSuggests the next item purchase based on the current game state.
  • Post-game summaryA recap screen shown once the game ends.
  • Jungle pathing on the minimapShows a suggested route directly on the in-game minimap.
  • Automatic updatesThe app updates itself; no manual download for new versions.
Stage 2 of 4 — built, switched off

Tempered

Finished and tested internally, kept behind a switch so nothing new appears until it's ready for everyone at once.

  • Staged rollout systemLets us turn a feature on for every player at the same time on the site and in the app, rather than a partial or silent release.
  • A handful of launch featuresIncluding an in-app currency, seasonal challenges, and daily quests — built, tested, and waiting for the switch.
Stage 3 of 4 — in progress

In the furnace

Actively being designed or built right now.

  • Personal coaching packsA paid service built around a custom champion kit and a live 1:1 review with a coach.
  • Clans & guildsLetting players group together, with a shared ranking.
  • "Legion" planA shared subscription for a group of five friends.
  • Match outcome predictionAn estimate of a game's odds while it's in progress.
  • Wider automated testingExtending our test coverage as the app grows.
  • Self-service billingLetting players cancel or change their card without contacting us directly.
Stage 4 of 4 — under consideration

Raw ore

Ideas we've discussed, with nothing built and no date attached.

  • TournamentsCosmetic rewards only — no entry fee and no cash prize, to stay within French gambling law.
  • Video / replay analysisReviewing a recorded game rather than a live one.
  • Beyond League of LegendsA long-term idea to support other Riot titles, starting with TFT.
